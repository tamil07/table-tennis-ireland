## Research and decisions

**Browser icon.** The Wix homepage shows a generic Wix icon in its page head. The round logo in the top left is the club's own image, "Leeside Logo 2025", stored on Wix. Decision: use the original logo file supplied by the owner, not a copy taken from the Wix site. Provide the standard set: a scalable SVG or 512-pixel PNG, a 180-pixel apple-touch icon, a 192-pixel and 512-pixel web app manifest icon, and a 32-pixel favicon.ico fallback.

**Closures and holidays.** The app already records closures with a start, an end, a reason and a reopen action, and notifies affected bookings. Decision: publish active closures in the public feed, show them on the home page and timetable, and mark closed days on the timetable. For search engines, express closures as opening-hours specifications with validFrom and validThrough dates, and keep the same wording visible on the page, since structured data must match what visitors see. Set the same closures as special hours in the Google Business Profile, which is the source Google uses in Search and Maps. Irish public holidays are entered by the owner each year, rather than imported automatically.

**Newsletter.** Irish rules require prior consent for marketing email under S.I. 336/2011, applied through the GDPR consent standard: freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous, with a clear affirmative action. The soft opt-in exception does not cover newsletter sign-ups. Decision: an unticked checkbox, a double opt-in confirmation email, the time, source and wording of each consent recorded, a working unsubscribe link in every message that does not require a login, and no pre-ticked boxes. Club service messages (bookings, memberships, safeguarding) are sent separately and are not marketing. Closure notices to subscribers are sent through the existing outbox and daily send cap.

**Map.** The free Google Maps embed is an iframe that needs no API key, but it contacts Google as soon as the page loads, which sends the visitor's IP address to Google before any consent. Decision: show the address as text with an "Open in Google Maps" link (no key, no contact with Google until clicked). Offer the embedded map as a click-to-load placeholder: the iframe is created only after the visitor clicks. Do not use the Maps Embed API, which needs an API key and billing. Update the privacy notice and the content security policy for the map frame. Legal advice is needed before relying on embedded maps for any club outside Leeside.

## Sources

- Google Maps embedding without an API key is described in [Google Maps Embed & iFrame generators](https://www.simplemaplab.com/tools/embed-map) and [a free iframe guide](https://www.mapsmaker.io/tools/embed-google-maps). These are third-party tools, so confirm the embed endpoint with Google before launch.
- Consent for embedded maps and third-party frames: [Google Maps and GDPR options](https://trustyourwebsite.com/nl/en/guides/google-maps-embed-gdpr) and [click-to-load guidance](https://devowl.io/gdpr-compliant/google-maps/).
- Irish email marketing rules: [Irish email marketing law overview](https://mailfois.com/email-marketing-guides/ireland-email-marketing-law-eprivacy/) and [Irish GDPR email questions](https://www.matrixinternet.ie/50-gdpr-email-marketing-questions-answered-for-ireland/). Check the Data Protection Commission's own guidance before launch.
- Holiday hours and structured data: [holiday hours markup guide](https://ryanbradley.com/blog/schema-markup-holiday-hours-special-hours/) and [OpenActive opening hours](https://developer.openactive.io/publishing-data/opening-hours). Confirm the Google-supported properties against Google's local business structured data documentation.
